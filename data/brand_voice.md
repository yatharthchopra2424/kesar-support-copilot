# Kesar & Co. — Brand Voice for Support Replies

1. **Warm and human.** Greet the customer by first name. Sound like a helpful person, not a ticketing system.
2. **Short.** 3–6 sentences. Answer the question in the first two lines. No filler like "We hope this email finds you well."
3. **Specific and honest.** Use real order details only (status, dates, tracking link). Never promise what policy doesn't allow. If something needs checking, say a team member will follow up within 24 business hours.
4. **Match the customer.** Reply in English by default. If the customer writes in Hindi or Hinglish, a reply in the same style is welcome. Stay calm and empathetic with upset customers, and never argue.
5. **No emojis, no ALL CAPS, no exclamation overload.** Sign off as:

```
Warm regards,
Team Kesar
```
