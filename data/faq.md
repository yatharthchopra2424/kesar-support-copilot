# Kesar & Co. — Customer Support FAQ & Policies

> This is the **only** source of truth for policy and product answers. If a question is not answered here, the copilot must not guess. Send it to a human.

Kesar & Co. is a D2C Ayurvedic skincare brand. We sell only on our own Shopify store (kesarandco.example) and ship **within India only**.

---

## 1. Shipping

- Orders are dispatched within **1–2 business days** (Mon–Sat, excluding public holidays).
- Delivery times after dispatch:
  - Metro cities (Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chennai, Kolkata, Hyderabad, Pune): **2–4 business days**
  - Rest of India: **4–6 business days**
  - North-East states, Jammu & Kashmir, Ladakh, Andaman & Nicobar, Lakshadweep: **6–8 business days**
- Shipping is **free on orders of ₹999 and above**. Below ₹999, a flat **₹79** shipping fee applies.
- We ship with our courier partners SwiftShip and BharatExpress. A tracking link is emailed when the order is dispatched.
- We do **not** ship outside India.
- We cannot change the delivery address once an order has been dispatched.

## 2. Cash on Delivery (COD)

- COD is available on orders up to **₹5,000**.
- A **₹49** COD handling fee applies. It is not refundable.
- Please keep exact change ready; delivery partners may not carry change.

## 3. Order Cancellation

- Orders can be cancelled **only before dispatch**. Once dispatched, the customer must wait for delivery and raise a return instead.
- If we cancel an order (for example, an item is out of stock), prepaid orders are refunded automatically to the original payment method within **5–7 business days**.

## 4. Returns

- Returns are accepted within **7 days of delivery**.
- Returned items must be **unused, unopened and with the seal intact**. For hygiene reasons, we cannot accept opened or used products, including "didn't suit me" or "didn't see results" cases.
- To start a return, the customer replies with the order ID. We arrange a free reverse pickup within 2–3 business days.
- Combo or kit products (e.g., Bridal Glow Kit) must be returned as a complete set.

## 5. Damaged, Leaking, Wrong or Missing Items

- Report within **48 hours of delivery**, with **photos** of the product and the outer box.
- After verification, we offer a **free replacement or a full refund** (customer's choice). The 7-day / unopened rule does **not** apply to these cases.
- Wrong item delivered: we pick up the wrong item and send the correct one at no cost.
- Missing item: we verify against the packing weight record and ship the missing item or refund its value.

## 6. Refunds

- **Prepaid orders:** refunded to the original payment method within **5–7 business days** after the returned item passes quality check.
- **COD orders:** refunded via **UPI or bank transfer**. We will ask the customer to share a UPI ID (or account holder name, account number and IFSC) **after** the pickup is completed.
- Shipping fees and COD fees are non-refundable, except for damaged, wrong or missing item cases where the full amount paid is refunded.
- We will **never** ask for an OTP, card PIN, CVV or password. Customers should not share these with anyone.

## 8. Product Safety & Medical Questions

- Our products are **cosmetics, not medicines**. They are not intended to diagnose, treat or cure any skin or medical condition.
- We always recommend a **patch test** (inner forearm, 24 hours) before first use.
- For any skin condition (e.g., eczema, psoriasis, severe acne), pregnancy or allergies, customers should **consult a dermatologist or doctor** before use.
- Support must **not** give medical advice or make claims about curing or treating conditions.
- If a customer reports a reaction, advise them to stop using the product immediately and consult a doctor, and escalate to a human.
